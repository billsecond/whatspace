# WhatSpace privacy statement

WhatSpace is a disk space analyzer that runs on your own computer. The project runs no service of
its own: there is no WhatSpace account and no WhatSpace server.

## What the project collects

Nothing. WhatSpace has no telemetry, no analytics, no crash reporting and no advertising, and it
does not check for updates. It has no network code at all.

## What WhatSpace reads

When you scan a drive or a folder, WhatSpace reads the names, sizes, dates and attributes of the
files and folders in it. It does not open or read what is inside your files. Online-only cloud
files (OneDrive and the like) are never opened, so a scan never downloads them.

What a scan finds stays in memory while the window is open. WhatSpace keeps only your choices
(units, theme, options) and the last few folders you scanned, in its settings on your computer.
An export (CSV or JSON) is written only where you save it.

## What WhatSpace changes

Nothing, unless you ask: "Move to Recycle Bin" or "Move to Trash" moves what you chose, after you
confirm, to your system's Recycle Bin or Trash, from where you can put it back. WhatSpace never
deletes anything itself.
